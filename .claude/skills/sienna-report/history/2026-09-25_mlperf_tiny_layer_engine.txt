MLPerf Tiny float models end to end on the SIENNA RTL, sienna_layer engine (2026-09-25)
Hardware schedules every layer; software writes each layer's configuration, streams its data in the fixed order
(im2col in software, as agreed) and checks the result. Verilator on the farm; time at an ASSUMED 950 MHz clock.
FLOPs count 2 per multiply-accumulate of the model's own layers (bias adds and ReLU not counted).
hw=float: hardware top-1 equals the float reference's. For ad01, an autoencoder, it is the worst relative
difference in anomaly score (mean squared reconstruction error) instead. hw=label: hardware top-1 equals the label.
worst err: largest per-layer max|hw-ref|/max|ref|. Cycles are identical for every input of a model: the schedule
does not depend on the data (ReLU and linear layers bypass the polynomial lanes, so no tail elements arise).
